Method & sources

How the comparisons are calculated

Only completed one-hour candles are used. Hour change is close/open − 1. Hour range is (high − low)/open. 24h change compares hourly closes exactly 24 hours apart. Relative volume and relative range divide the current hour by the median for the same UTC hour on the previous seven days.

London time follows daylight saving where timezone data is available. The UTC baseline is fixed; around a clock change it can correspond to a different local hour. Seven observations provide descriptive context, not statistical significance.

Binance spot candles only. USDT quotes are not USD prices. These figures cannot explain why prices moved or describe every exchange. Missing or stale pairs are excluded.
