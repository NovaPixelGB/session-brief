How the comparisons are calculated

Only completed one-hour candles are used. Hour change is close/open − 1. Hour range is (high − low)/open. 24h change compares hourly closes exactly 24 hours apart. Relative volume and relative range divide the current hour by the median for the same UTC hour on the previous seven days.

London time follows daylight saving where the runtime has timezone data. The UTC baseline is fixed; around a clock change it can correspond to a different local hour. Seven samples are descriptive context, not evidence of statistical significance.

Sources and limitations

Binance spot candles only. USDT quotes are not USD prices. This brief cannot explain why prices moved or describe every exchange. Missing or stale pairs are excluded.
